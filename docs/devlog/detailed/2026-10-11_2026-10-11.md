## The mesh is complete and the pipeline's grid matches it to 0.01 percent

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-11 01:10–08:46

**Action:** `scripts/fetch-model.mjs` and `scripts/build-grid.mjs` fetch the Scan the World STL from Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0, 59,997,484 bytes, SHA-1 checked against the Commons API), normalise it to 5.17 m facing +z, check it, render silhouettes, sample a signed distance grid on 32 threads and write the Int16 grid file.

**Result:** 1,199,948 triangles, watertight with consistent windings (every edge on exactly two faces), figure 2.158 × 5.170 × 1.489 m, grid volume within 0.01 percent of the mesh's divergence-theorem volume, deterministic SHA-256 over two runs, 11 s at 8 mm. The silhouettes show the rock base, the sling down the back, the stump behind the right leg, the whole back and the top of the head. The first build at band 32 gzipped to 31 MB; band 16 (still above the largest margin plus the deepest cut) gave 17 MB.

**Reasoning:** The research's Int8 narrow band was rejected for the figure grid because the carving rule `max(stock, min(-tool, figure - margin))` needs the figure's true distance out to margin plus cut depth; the band was then sized from that, not from memory.

**Validation:** 94 unit tests including the writer against `src/contracts/grid.ts`; the coordinator looked at the silhouettes. Not proved: the engine's reader on this file (integration).
