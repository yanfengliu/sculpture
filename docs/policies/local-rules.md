# Local rules — sculpture

Rules that bind only in this repo, alongside the fleet canon: its core in AGENTS.md, and the rest in `../fleet/docs/skills/coordinator.md` and `../fleet/docs/work-rules.md`. The canon sync never opens this file.

- No person appears (owner, 2026-10-11). Only the tools, the stock and what comes off it are shown. A tool moves as if held and struck: it rests where a sculptor would set it down, never floats idle in frame, never clips through stock, and never cuts where a sculptor could not reach or see.
- Everything else is authentic (owner, 2026-10-11): the material and how it breaks, the kinds, sizes and names of the tools, the order of the work and the figure's real form and size. Where the record is silent the choice follows the period's documented practice and is written down with its reason in `docs/research/`. A departure from the record is a defect, not a style.
- Every stage names its sources: a step's label cites the research note behind it, and a modelling or choreography change names the reference images and texts it was compared with.
- The making is the product: a feature that exists only in the finished state and never arrives through a tool is a defect.
- Nothing stands between the camera and the stock (owner, 2026-10-11: "the fences are blocking the view"). The plank enclosure, the scaffold spans and the props that lie between the camera and the block are hidden while the far ones stay, from every preset and while orbiting, and a view toggle hides the yard structures entirely. The yard is context for the making, never an obstacle to watching it.
